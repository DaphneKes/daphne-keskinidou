About

I'm Daphne Keskinidou. I draw in Athens.

I studied Illustration at Ornerakis Applied Arts, then Branding & Rebranding at EKPA. Somewhere along the way, I stopped being able to draw anything without also thinking about how it'll print, how it'll scale, or how it'll hold up on a shirt. That's just how my brain works now.

I like drawing people mid-moment, not posed.

Everything's ink and flat color. Five colors max. Minimal gradients. No shortcuts. Paper or iPad, doesn't matter. It's still my hand doing it.

I'm not chasing one look or one client. I just like drawing the stuff around me and making it look a little louder than it actually was.
